关于我的研究

About My Research

我的研究将物理模型与数据驱动方法结合，目标是在保证预测性能的同时，让深度学习模型能够服务于真实引力波观测中的快速推断、高精度分析和物理解释。研究对象主要包括双中子星和中等质量黑洞并合等致密双星系统。

My research combines physics-based modeling with data-driven inference, aiming to make deep learning useful for fast inference, high-precision analysis, and physical interpretation in gravitational-wave observations. I mainly work on compact binary systems including binary neutron stars and intermediate-mass black hole mergers.

01

引力波机器学习

Deep Learning for Gravitational Waves

面向信号识别、分类、去噪和快速推断，构建适合引力波时间序列与参数空间的深度学习模型。

Developing deep learning models for signal identification, classification, denoising, and fast inference in gravitational-wave time series and parameter spaces.

02

双中子星波形建模

Binary Neutron Star Waveform Modeling

研究潮汐效应、进动效应与精细波形结构，探索条件自编码器等模型在 BNS 波形生成中的表达能力。

Studying tidal effects, precession effects, and fine waveform structures, with conditional autoencoders and related models for BNS waveform generation.

03

参数估计与中子星物理

Parameter Estimation and Neutron-Star Physics

围绕参数后验、退化关系和不确定性表征，从潮汐参数、g-mode 共振和状态方程中提取物理信息。

Extracting physical information from tidal parameters, g-mode resonance, and equation-of-state signatures through posterior inference and uncertainty modeling.
