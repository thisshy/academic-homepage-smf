孙孟飞 Mengfei Sun / Mengfei Sun

博士研究生，重庆大学

PhD Researcher, Chongqing University

我主要研究机器学习在引力波天体物理中的应用，重点关注双中子星（BNS）和中等质量黑洞并合等致密双星系统的引力波数据分析问题，工作内容包括波形生成、波形重构与去噪、信号识别与分类、参数估计，以及潮汐效应、g-mode 共振和状态方程信息提取等精细物理效应建模。

I study machine learning methods for gravitational-wave astrophysics, with emphasis on compact binary systems such as binary neutron stars and intermediate-mass black hole mergers. My work covers waveform generation, waveform reconstruction and denoising, signal identification and classification, parameter estimation, and physically motivated modeling of tidal effects, g-mode resonance, and equation-of-state information.

01

研究定位

Research Focus

我的研究将物理建模与数据驱动方法结合，目标是在保证预测性能的同时，让深度学习模型能够服务于真实引力波观测中的快速推断、高精度分析和物理解释。

My research combines physics-based modeling with data-driven inference, aiming to make deep learning useful for fast inference, high-precision analysis, and physical interpretation in gravitational-wave observations.

02

方法体系

Methods

常用模型包括 CNN、ResNet、条件自编码器、Transformer 和概率模型，并根据不同任务设计面向波形、参数空间和不确定性表征的网络结构。

I work with CNNs, ResNets, conditional autoencoders, Transformers, and probabilistic models, adapting network architectures to waveform modeling, parameter-space inference, and uncertainty representation.

03

当前关注

Current Topics

当前重点关注 BNS 波形生成与重构、中等质量黑洞并合参数估计、多频段透镜化引力波识别，以及中子星内部物理相关效应的信息提取。

Current topics include BNS waveform generation and reconstruction, parameter estimation for intermediate-mass black hole binaries, multiband lensed gravitational-wave detection, and neutron-star internal-physics inference.

Research

研究方向

Research Directions

波形生成、重构与去噪

Waveform Generation, Reconstruction, and Denoising

利用深度学习模型学习致密双星波形族的低维结构，实现快速波形生成、信号重构和噪声背景下的稳健恢复。

Learning compact representations of waveform families for fast generation, signal reconstruction, and robust recovery under noisy observational conditions.

参数估计与不确定性量化

Parameter Estimation and Uncertainty Quantification

面向 BNS 和中等质量黑洞并合，构建快速参数估计流程，并关注后验结构、退化关系和模型不确定性的表达。

Developing fast inference workflows for BNS and intermediate-mass black hole mergers, with attention to posterior structure, degeneracies, and model uncertainty.

信号识别、分类与多频段探测

Signal Identification, Classification, and Multiband Detection

研究深度学习在引力波信号识别、随机引力波背景分类、多频段透镜化信号探测等任务中的应用。

Applying deep learning to gravitational-wave signal recognition, stochastic background classification, and multiband detection of lensed events.

潮汐效应、g-mode 共振与状态方程

Tidal Effects, g-mode Resonance, and Equation of State

围绕中子星内部物理建立可解释的机器学习分析流程，从精细波形效应中提取潮汐参数、共振特征和状态方程信息。

Building interpretable machine-learning workflows to extract tidal parameters, resonance features, and equation-of-state information from subtle waveform effects.

Projects

研究项目

Research Projects

01

BNS 波形生成与重构

BNS Waveform Generation and Reconstruction

围绕潮汐效应、进动效应与波形族快速生成，探索条件自编码器等模型在高维波形空间中的表达能力。

Exploring conditional autoencoders and related models for fast BNS waveform generation in the presence of tidal and precession effects.

02

中等质量黑洞并合参数估计

Intermediate-Mass Binary Black Hole Inference

面向 DECIGO 等空间引力波探测场景，研究中等质量双黑洞并合信号的快速参数估计与探测能力。

Studying fast parameter estimation and detection capability for intermediate-mass binary black hole mergers in space-based detector scenarios such as DECIGO.

03

多频段透镜化引力波识别

Multiband Lensed Gravitational-Wave Detection

结合多频段观测与深度学习分类模型，识别由暗物质晕等结构引起的透镜化引力波特征。

Combining multiband observations with deep-learning classifiers to identify lensed gravitational-wave features induced by structures such as dark matter halos.
