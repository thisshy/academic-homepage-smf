研究 Research

从波形到物理解释

From waveforms to physical interpretation

以物理模型约束数据驱动方法，使快速推断不仅追求精度，也能服务于真实观测和中子星内部物理研究。

Physics-informed data-driven methods for fast, accurate, and interpretable analysis of real gravitational-wave observations.

01

波形生成、重构与去噪

Waveform Generation, Reconstruction, and Denoising

利用深度学习模型学习致密双星波形族的低维结构，实现快速波形生成、信号重构和噪声背景下的稳健恢复。

Learning compact representations of waveform families for fast generation, signal reconstruction, and robust recovery under noisy observational conditions.

02

参数估计与不确定性量化

Parameter Estimation and Uncertainty Quantification

面向 BNS、NSBH 和中等质量黑洞并合构建快速参数估计流程，关注后验结构、参数退化关系和模型不确定性的表达。

Developing fast inference workflows for BNS, NSBH, and intermediate-mass black hole mergers, with attention to posterior structure, degeneracies, and model uncertainty.

03

信号识别、多频段探测与背景分析

Signal Identification, Multiband Detection, and Backgrounds

研究引力波信号识别、多频段透镜化信号探测和随机引力波背景相关的数据分析问题，关注观测条件和算法稳健性。

Studying signal identification, multiband detection of lensed events, and data-analysis problems related to stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds.

04

中子星内部物理与状态方程

Neutron-Star Internal Physics and Equation of State

围绕中子星内部物理建立可解释的机器学习分析流程，从精细波形效应中提取潮汐参数和状态方程信息。

Building interpretable machine-learning workflows to extract tidal parameters and equation-of-state information from subtle waveform effects.
