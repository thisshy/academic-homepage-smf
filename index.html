01

研究定位

Research Focus

研究以双黑洞、双中子星等双星系统的引力波数据为主要对象，结合物理建模与数据驱动方法，服务于真实观测场景中的信号分析、快速推断和物理解释。

The research centers on gravitational-wave data from binary black hole, binary neutron star, and related binary systems, combining physics-based modeling with data-driven methods for signal analysis, fast inference, and physical interpretation.

02

方法体系

Methods

方法上关注深度学习在天文数据中的应用，包括面向时间序列、波形数据和多源观测数据的特征学习、分类识别、重构建模与统计推断。

Methodologically, the work emphasizes deep learning for astronomical data, including feature learning, classification, reconstruction, modeling, and statistical inference for time series, waveform data, and multi-source observations.

03

当前关注

Current Topics

当前关注双星系统引力波数据分析与深度学习天文应用之间的交叉问题，重点放在可用于真实观测数据处理的模型、流程和解释方法上。

Current topics focus on the intersection between gravitational-wave data analysis for binary systems and deep-learning applications in astronomy, with emphasis on models, workflows, and interpretability for real observational data.

Research

研究方向

Research Directions

波形生成、重构与去噪

Waveform Generation, Reconstruction, and Denoising

利用深度学习模型学习致密双星波形族的低维结构，实现快速波形生成、信号重构和噪声背景下的稳健恢复。

Learning compact representations of waveform families for fast generation, signal reconstruction, and robust recovery under noisy observational conditions.

参数估计与不确定性量化

Parameter Estimation and Uncertainty Quantification

面向 BNS 和中等质量黑洞并合，构建快速参数估计流程，并关注后验结构、退化关系和模型不确定性的表达。

Developing fast inference workflows for BNS and intermediate-mass black hole mergers, with attention to posterior structure, degeneracies, and model uncertainty.

信号识别、分类与多频段探测

Signal Identification, Classification, and Multiband Detection

研究深度学习在引力波信号识别、随机引力波背景分类、多频段透镜化信号探测等任务中的应用。

Applying deep learning to gravitational-wave signal recognition, stochastic background classification, and multiband detection of lensed events.

潮汐效应、g-mode 共振与状态方程

Tidal Effects, g-mode Resonance, and Equation of State

围绕中子星内部物理建立可解释的机器学习分析流程，从精细波形效应中提取潮汐参数、共振特征和状态方程信息。

Building interpretable machine-learning workflows to extract tidal parameters, resonance features, and equation-of-state information from subtle waveform effects.
